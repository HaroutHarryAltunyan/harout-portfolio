About

I'm a founder and engineer who builds whole products: the architecture, the code, the infrastructure and the brand around them.

Today I'm Co-Founder and CTO of SAECULA, an AI health and longevity platform. I lead its architecture across web, mobile, backend, AI agents and cloud. Before that I started HyeLynk, a social commerce platform for the Armenian diaspora, which taught me how to design large distributed systems, recommendation feeds, content moderation and blockchain rewards.

I came into software through UC Berkeley Extension's full-stack program in 2024 and kept going through independent work in security research, machine learning and distributed systems. I care about privacy and safety by design, and about technology that makes healthcare more human.
